How to Calculate Selling Price and Profit Margin in Nigeria (Margin vs Markup, Break-even)

Set a selling price that really keeps your profit: margin vs markup with Naira examples, and how many units you must sell each month to break even.

A price that matches the shop next door can still lose money if your costs are higher. Know your own numbers.

The cost-plus-margin method

  1. Find what one unit costs you: the purchase price plus any transport you pay to bring it in, divided by the number of units.
  2. Decide the gross margin you want, for example 25%.
  3. Divide the cost by (1 − margin) to get the price.

Fictional example: A carton of a drink costs ₦6,000 and you want a 25% gross margin. Price = 6,000 ÷ 0.75 = ₦8,000. Check: profit is ₦2,000, and 2,000 ÷ 8,000 = 0.25.

Margin is not markup. Adding 25% on cost (6,000 × 1.25 = ₦7,500) gives profit of ₦1,500, a margin of only 1,500 ÷ 7,500 = 20%.

Cover your running costs

Gross profit must be bigger than Total Expenses, or you lose money.

Break-even units = monthly expenses ÷ profit per unit.

Fictional example: Expenses are ₦90,000 a month. A carton sells at ₦8,000 and costs ₦6,000, so each earns ₦2,000. Break-even = 90,000 ÷ 2,000 = 45 cartons. Carton 46 begins to pay you. For many products, use your top seller for a rough target.

Pricing habits that help

  • Do not discount below cost. Check the cost before agreeing a "last price".
  • Be careful with small margins on heavy, slow-moving goods; your money sits on the shelf.
  • Push the products that pay you better.

Quick tips

Pricing tip for traders: margin is not markup. A carton costs you ₦6,000. If you add 25% you sell at ₦7,500, but your profit of ₦1,500 is only 20% of the price. To really keep 25 kobo in every naira, divide the cost by 0.75: ₦8,000. Small difference per carton, big difference by month end.

Do you know how many units you must sell before the shop pays you? Monthly expenses ÷ profit per unit = break-even. Example: ₦90,000 of expenses (rent, fuel, transport, helper, data). Each carton earns you ₦2,000. 90,000 ÷ 2,000 = 45 cartons. Carton 46 is the first one that pays you.
