Small Business Money Terms Explained: Revenue, Profit Margin, Break-even, Drawings

Plain-English definitions of revenue, cost of goods sold, gross and net profit, markup, break-even and owner's drawings, for Nigerian traders.

  • Revenue: total money from sales before any costs.
  • Cost of Goods Sold: unit cost multiplied by quantity sold.
  • Gross Profit: Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold.
  • Gross Margin: Gross Profit ÷ Revenue.
  • Operating Expenses: running costs such as rent, fuel, transport and wages.
  • Net Profit: Gross Profit − Total Expenses.
  • Markup: the amount added on top of cost to make the price.
  • Break-even: where profit from sales exactly covers running costs.
  • POS: point-of-sale card payment machine.
  • Debtor / Creditor: someone who owes you / someone you owe.
  • Drawings: money the owner takes from the business for personal use.
