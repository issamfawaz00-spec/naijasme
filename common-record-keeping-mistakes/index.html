10 Record-Keeping Mistakes Nigerian Small Businesses Make (and How to Fix Them)

From forgetting transport, data and POS charges to treating credit sales as cash: the mistakes that make your profit look wrong.

Common mistakes

  1. Recording only some sales. Small sales add up.
  2. Typing the total as the unit price.
  3. Leaving unit cost blank or out of date. Profit will be wrong.
  4. Forgetting small expenses like transport, data and POS charges.
  5. Mixing personal and business money.
  6. Treating credit sales as cash.
  7. Never counting stock, or never backing up. Count the shelf monthly. Copy the file to a second place weekly.

Quick tips

300 a day doesn't sound like much. Over a 30-day month it's ₦9,000. Transport, data, POS charges and generator fuel: if you don't write them down, your profit looks bigger than it is. Keep the receipt, or write one line in your notebook the same day.

Month-end checklist for small shops: All sales written down? Small expenses too? Shelf count matches your records? Customers owing followed up? Real cash, bank and POS balances checked? Paid yourself only the fixed amount?
